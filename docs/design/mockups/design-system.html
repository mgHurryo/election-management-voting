3 · Buttons

Primary = vermilion solid. Ghost = ink border. Quiet = underline hairline. Disabled = 50% opacity, no cursor change.

4 · Status Dots

Open Draft Closed

Dot + word, never color alone. Open = vermilion, Draft = bone outline, Closed = soft gray.

12 · Empty & Error States

EMPTY

No elections to show.

No candidates yet.

ERROR

Unable to reach the server. Check your network or backend service.

Errors always pair the message with a Retry action. A timeout never implies failure: "A timeout does not mean the operation failed."

13 · Motion & Accessibility Floor

View transitions: fade + 4px rise, 200ms ease-out — skipped under prefers-reduced-motion. Focus: 2px ink outline, offset 2px. Touch targets ≥ 44px. Radios are visually hidden but remain focusable and labelled. Links: underlined on hover, tab order follows DOM. All copy is notice-tone English; no slogans, no time bounds, no blanket voting rules.
